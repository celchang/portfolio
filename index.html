Celine Chang

Hi, I’m Celine, a New York–based product designer.

I’ve spent over a decade designing consumer products at companies of every stage, including Wonder, Luminary, BuzzFeed and Compass. Before moving into tech, I spent several years building brands and digital experiences across industries at Co-op Brand Partners.

I’m fascinated by the space between behavioral psychology and design: how a small nudge, a well-placed bit of friction or a simpler set of choices changes what people actually do. I believe function and simplicity almost always win, but there’s always room for delight in the details.
